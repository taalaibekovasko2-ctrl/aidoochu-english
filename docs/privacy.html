Privacy Policy / Купуялуулук саясаты

Айдоочунун англисчеси (Aidoochu English) · Updated: November 15, 2026

English

This app helps truck drivers learn English. It does not require an account and does not collect, sell or share personal information.

  • All learning progress and settings are stored only on your device (browser local storage).
  • The app creates a random device ID on your phone. It is used only to check your activation code on the device and is never sent to us automatically.
  • Text-to-speech uses your phone's built-in voice engine.
  • The "Truck shop" search opens Google Maps in your browser; Google's own privacy policy applies there.
  • The app contains no ads. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visit counter that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data, only to count page visits.
